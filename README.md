# Bible Flow - Chrome Extension

A simple Chrome extension for instant Hindi Bible verse lookup. Perfect for churches conducting online services.

## Features

- **Hindi BSI Version** - Complete Hindi Bible with accurate translations
- **Easy Navigation** - Select Testament → Book → Chapter → Verses
- **One-Click Copy** - Copy selected verses to clipboard instantly
- **Dark/Light Theme** - Toggle between themes for comfortable reading
- **Recent History** - Keep track of recently copied verses

## Installation

### Step 1: Download the Extension

1. Download the `bible-flow.7z` file from this repository
2. Extract the contents to a folder on your computer

### Step 2: Install in Chrome

1. Open Google Chrome
2. Navigate to `chrome://extensions/`
3. Enable **Developer mode** (toggle in the top right corner)
4. Click **Load unpacked**
5. Select the extracted `bible-flow` folder
6. The extension icon will appear in your Chrome toolbar

## How to Use

### Opening the Extension

Click the extension icon (✝) in your Chrome toolbar to open the Bible popup.

### Finding a Verse

1. **Select Testament** - Choose Old Testament or New Testament
2. **Select Book** - Choose a book (e.g., यूहन्ना/John, भजन संहिता/Psalms)
3. **Select Chapter** - Choose the chapter number
4. **Select Verses** - Check the verses you want to copy

### Copying Verses

- Simply check the verses you want - they copy automatically to your clipboard
- Or click "Select All" to copy the entire chapter
- Paste anywhere (chat, document, notes)

### Using in Online Church

1. Open the extension during your online church service
2. Find the verse you want to share
3. Select the verse(s) - they copy automatically
4. Paste in chat, screen share, or document sharing

## For Church Leaders

This extension is ideal for:
- Online Sunday services
- Bible study sessions
- Youth groups
- Prayer meetings
- Any virtual church event

Simply find the verse and copy to share with your congregation!

## Theme

Click the moon/sun icon in the header to switch between dark and light themes. Your preference is saved automatically.
